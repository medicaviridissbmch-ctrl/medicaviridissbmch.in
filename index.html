MEDICA
VIRIDIS

Future doctors for a greener campus.

Medica Viridis brings MBBS students together to explore how the environment shapes health, starting on our own campus. Members plant and care for greenery, explore environmental questions through research, and share what they learn through presentations and awareness campaigns. There’s a place for students who enjoy gardening, writing, photography, or organising events.
